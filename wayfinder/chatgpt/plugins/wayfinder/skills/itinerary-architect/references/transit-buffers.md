# Travel estimates

For a first pass, budget roughly 3 km/h relaxed, 4 km/h moderate, or 5 km/h brisk
on level paths, before stops. These are adjustable planning estimates, not step
limits or accessibility guarantees. User mobility constraints take priority.
Group nearby stops, but verify crossings and entrances rather than imposing a
fixed radius as proof of walkability. Add time for stairs, hills, crowds and rests.

For transit, separately estimate access, waiting, rides and transfers. With no
verified timetable, mark the estimate and allow a flexible 10–20-minute buffer.
Timed tickets and departures need extra contingency appropriate to consequences.
Do not invent departure times or assume frequent service. Trim optional stops
before compressing meals, rest or necessary buffers.
