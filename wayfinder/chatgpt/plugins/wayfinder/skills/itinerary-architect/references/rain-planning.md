# Weather and indoor swaps

Use forecast dates actually returned by the provider. If precipitation probability
is 50% or higher, flag the outdoor block and suggest a nearby indoor option.
Probability is not rainfall duration or intensity. Check severe conditions
separately using available sources. Outside the forecast window, offer conditional
backups rather than claiming a forecast. Mention provider and retrieval/source date.

Keep the original plan intact during an audit. Compare primary/backup stops,
extra travel, hours, costs and required tickets. Prefer a museum, covered market,
or cafe in the same neighborhood, but verify hours and accessibility. Do not
silently save changes to a trip or file.
